Forward-Deployed AI Engineering

Forward-deployed engineering brings technical specialists directly into the customer’s operational environment. The work starts with a measurable business problem, then integrates data, models, workflows and approvals into a system people can use.

Discuss a Deployment →

What an FDE engagement includes

Operational discovery, architecture and access assessment, a bounded scope of work, integration and acceptance testing, and a release and operations plan.

Where FDE adds value

Knowledge workflows, cross-system approvals, document processing, operational copilots, secure AI automation and modernization.

How we establish quality

Technical validation, data classification, human control over consequential actions, explicit acceptance criteria and monitoring.

Engagement model

Paid discovery can lead to a production sprint, a defined deployment or embedded engineering capacity. Every proposal is scoped for the client.

Frequently asked questions

What is forward-deployed engineering?

An embedded engineering approach that connects AI capabilities to an organization’s real systems, people and constraints.

Does DLI replace our technology team?

No. The intention is to work alongside the existing team with shared ownership, knowledge transfer and agreed responsibilities.
