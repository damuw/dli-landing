Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information Deep Learning Intelligence (“DLI”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use deeplearningintelligence.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Deep Learning Intelligence provides forward-deployed AI engineering and AI-native product development. You can contact us about privacy at damu@DeepLearningIntelligence.com.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details you submit. When you use the deployment brief form we collect your name, work email, organization, country or market, the capability you are interested in, timeline, and the description of the problem you provide.
  • Research requests. When you request a briefing, we collect your email address and the resource you selected so we can send you the download link.
  • Readiness assessment. The assessment answers and the indicative profile are calculated in your browser. If you choose to request a confidential review, we receive your answers, the resulting profile and the contact details you provide so we can prepare the conversation. Do not enter confidential system details, credentials or personal data in any field.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and security providers process standard server logs such as IP address, browser user agent, requested URL and timestamps in order to deliver and protect the site.
  • Preferences. Your cookie choice and accessibility settings are stored in your browser (see the Cookie Notice).

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a relevant first conversation.
  • To send the research briefing you asked for.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To measure how the site is used — only where you have given consent.

Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional measurement, our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and keeping the site secure, and the performance of a contract where we are discussing or providing services.

Cookies and measurement

We use a small number of first-party cookies and browser storage entries to remember your consent and accessibility preferences. We do not currently run third-party advertising cookies. Any optional usage measurement will only run after you accept the cookie notice. Details are set out in the Cookie Notice.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site and communicate with you — for example our hosting provider, our email service, and infrastructure providers — under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection commitments, or where required by law.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept while we are in contact and for up to 24 months after our last interaction, unless a longer period is required for a contractual relationship, dispute resolution or legal obligation. Consent and accessibility preferences remain in your browser until you clear them or they expire.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to request portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email damu@DeepLearningIntelligence.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United Arab Emirates, the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States or other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The revised version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.

This page is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice. Contracting entities and jurisdiction-specific terms are confirmed in each engagement.
