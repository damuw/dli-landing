UAE & GCC · Why now

The mandate is here. Delivery is the constraint.

Three signals define the current window: a national agentic-AI mandate, sovereign compute being built, and institutional capital moving into AI operating infrastructure. Each one raises the same question — who can turn it into a governed operating capability?

Read this correctly

These references are market context, not endorsements. DLI is not a government partner, holds no regulatory approvals, and is not an MGX portfolio company. The MGX fund is an investment vehicle, not a procurement budget available to DLI.

Sources and dates are listed with each signal. Every page that cites a public claim carries a review date and links to the primary source.

01 · AGENTIC GOVERNMENT APR–MAY 2026

From ambition to a federal implementation framework.

In April 2026 the UAE set an objective to transition 50% of government services and operations to agentic AI within two years. On 18 May 2026 the Cabinet approved a federal framework, a first package of services across four categories, and a programme to train 80,000 federal employees.

What DLI delivers: service-journey discovery, identity and consent design, exception and appeal paths, Arabic/English evaluation, and procurement-ready decision artifacts.

Sources: UAE Cabinet, 23 Apr 2026 · Government of Dubai Media Office, 18 May 2026

02 · SOVEREIGN COMPUTE PLANNED

Capacity is being built. Portability still matters.

Stargate UAE is announced as a 1 GW cluster within a 5 GW UAE–US AI campus, with the first 200 MW expected to come online in 2026. It is planned infrastructure, not an operational claim. More capacity changes the build/buy calculus; it does not remove the need for cost, latency and exit discipline.

What DLI delivers: deployment options across in-country, regional and global environments; model routing and fallback; unit-economics modelling; vendor-portability reviews.

Source: G42, 22 May 2025

03 · INSTITUTIONAL CAPITAL 1 JUL 2026

The question moves from models to operating models.

MGX confirmed the final close of Fund I at $49 billion in total commitments on 1 July 2026, above its $45 billion target. For institutional investors, diligence now has to cover data control, compute economics, management ownership and the path from capex to operating value — not only model capability.

What DLI delivers: AI diligence frameworks, portfolio-intelligence architecture, data-control reviews and operating-value plans.

Source: MGX, 1 Jul 2026
