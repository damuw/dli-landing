This notice explains the cookies and browser storage used on deeplearningintelligence.com, and how you can control them.

Your choice

The first time you visit, a notice explains what we use and asks you to accept. When you accept, we remember your choice and the notice is not shown again. You can change your mind at any time by clearing site data in your browser, or by visiting this link to reset the notice.

What we use

  • Consent preference — a first-party cookie named dli_consent (up to 12 months) and a local storage entry named dli_cookie_consent, used to remember that you accepted the notice. These are strictly necessary for the notice to work.
  • Accessibility preferences — a local storage entry named dli_a11y, used to remember your text size, contrast, motion and link settings.

These entries are stored by the Site itself. They are not advertising cookies and are not used to build profiles across other websites.

Measurement

If we enable optional usage measurement, it will only run after you accept the notice, and we will update this page to describe the provider and retention period. We do not currently run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Third-party destinations

Some links open external sites (for example GoFundItNow, Any.xyz and help.ai). Those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies; we do not control them.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary entries will not break the Site, but the cookie notice may reappear on each visit. Browser help pages explain how to manage site data for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.
